Pseudonymize

    Vault

    Pseudonymize swaps each value for a marker such as [[EMAIL_7KQ4ZM]], and keeps the original encrypted in this vault, on this device. The vault locks after 15 minutes without use, or when Chrome closes.

    A remembered device unlocks the vault without your password for 30 days. Locking by hand asks for your password next time.
