Pseudonymize

    Vault

    Pseudonymize swaps each value for a marker such as [[EMAIL_7KQ4ZM]], and keeps the original encrypted in this vault, on this device. The vault locks when Chrome closes, or after 15 minutes without use unless this device is remembered.

    A remembered device keeps the vault unlocked, and unlocks it without your password, for 30 days. Locking by hand asks for your password next time. If you forget your password, you can only delete the vault.

    If you forget your password, no one can open your vault, DataFog included. You can only delete it and start over.

    Deleting your vault removes every original value in it. Markers you've already used can't be restored. If you've forgotten your password, this is the only way to start over.

    Restore

    Values marked not sensitive

    Mark a value Not sensitive in a review, and DataFog leaves it as is from then on. It's left out of reviews and, while the vault is unlocked, the badge and Redact skip it too.

    These values are saved in your vault, on this device. Remove one to have DataFog replace it again.
